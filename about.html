Why Camouflage to Canvas exists

Marine. Artist. Lupus Warrior. Unfettered.

I'm Jeanette Pizarro-Harpe. I know what it's like to carry things the world can't see. I also know that even in the darkest seasons, beauty persists. This studio is where I share what a paintbrush taught me.

"I'm not a therapist. I'm a Marine, an artist, and a woman who found her way back to herself through a paintbrush. Here, I hold the space. You do the healing, in your own way, at your own pace."

Jeanette Pizarro-HarpeFounder of Camouflage to Canvas · Marine Corps Veteran · Certified Art Healing Facilitator
Chapter one

From the Bronx to the Corps

My life has been a collection of many different landscapes. I grew up on the busy streets of the Bronx, rooted in my Puerto Rican heritage and raised by a Marine father. In 1991, I earned my own Eagle, Globe and Anchor and served in the U.S. Marine Corps until 1997.

After the Corps, I kept serving, spending years as a federal civilian employee with the Department of Veterans Affairs and the Department of the Interior's U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service. I've spent most of my life learning what it means to be a warrior.

Chapter two

Living behind the camouflage

For a long time, I wore camouflage long after I took off the uniform. I'm a disabled veteran and I live with lupus, an illness most people never see. Some days that means pain and exhaustion. Many days it meant smiling through things I didn't have words for.

When my health led me to retire early from federal service in 2021, I had to ask a hard question: who am I now?

Chapter three

Finding my way back to the canvas

The answer was waiting where it had always been: in color. I'd studied digital arts and multimedia design, and for more than 20 years I worked as a designer. But it was oil, acrylic and watercolor, and the patient Old Master techniques I love, that gave me back to myself.

Through painting, I learned to shed labels, process what I'd carried, and live unfettered. I draw inspiration from Van Gogh's emotion, O'Keeffe's eye for nature and Monet's light, and from my faith, which has carried me through every season.

Camouflage to Canvas is the table I wish I'd found sooner: a warm, judgment-free place where you don't have to explain yourself, and you don't have to know how to paint.

"Through the fluid grace of the canvas, I discovered how to live unfettered, free from the labels and the pain."

What the name means

Camouflage

What we wear to get through. The brave face, the "I'm fine," the weight we carry so no one has to see it. It kept many of us safe. It isn't meant to be worn forever.

Canvas

The open space where you can finally set it down. A place to make a mess, let color say what words can't, and paint something new. Your past doesn't have to be your permanent landscape.

My role in the room

A facilitator and a fellow traveler, not a therapist.

I'm a Certified Art Healing Facilitator. I guide the creative process, hold a safe and respectful space, and witness what you choose to share. I don't diagnose, treat or provide therapy. Our experiences are meant to walk alongside, never replace, professional care.
