Why Camouflage to Canvas exists

I know what it's like to carry things the world can't see.

I'm Jeanette, a U.S. Marine veteran, a Lupus Warrior, and an artist. For years I lived behind camouflage. Painting is where I learned to set some of that weight down, one brushstroke at a time.

Camouflage to Canvas is the table I wish I'd found sooner: a warm, judgment-free place where you don't have to explain yourself, and you don't have to know how to paint.

I wore camouflage long after I took off the uniform. Painting is how I took it off.
Jeanette Pizarro-HarpeFounder & Certified Art Healing FacilitatorMarine Corps Veteran · Artist · Fellow Traveler
